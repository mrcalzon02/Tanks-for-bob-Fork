# Lumberyard V2 — boiler-powered primitive belt-driven timber saw

User-approved concept supersedes the proposed wedge splitter. The central
machine must visibly read as **Franklin-style cast-iron stove / copper boiler
→ exposed steam piston and flywheel → moving leather belt → rotating steel saw**.

The boiler burns visibly inside an enclosed stove, with grate/door/chimney and
a short horizontal pressure vessel. The flywheel turns the drive pulley; a
continuous visible belt runs to the saw spindle. The saw is a true thick
toothed disk (not a modern electric motor). The moving log is cut LONGWAYS
into two half-trunk meshes with paler flat-cut faces and then mechanically
ejected into a stack of lengthwise-halved logs.

Separate editable geometry: input pile of six bark-covered full round trunks,
stove casting, fire door and grate, boiler and pipe, small engine cylinder,
five-spoke flywheel, drive pulleys and leather belt, shaft and toothed saw,
log carriage, six finished D-profile split half logs, moving hero log and
two ejected halves, wood chip burst. All use the fixed 45-degree structure
camera and four independent model rotations.

Working loop: 16 frames @ 8fps — boiler glow, rotating engine/flywheel and
belt stitching, saw disk spinning, active log advancing, lengthwise split,
the two halves exiting toward the finished pile, reset. Idle is cold and
stationary. The original Factorio `assembling-machine/growing-plot` prototype
and gameplay are NOT modified until visual approval.

CI: `.github/workflows/lumberyard-v2.yml`. Actual saved Blender source and
all 68 RGBA renders publish to
`models/island-survival/lumberyard/generated-v2/`.
